  A property tax calculator and 3D tax-burden map for Gallatin County, Montana. Yellowstone County is being added. Computes each parcel's annual property tax from Montana DOR and cadastral data, and matches real county tax bills to the cent. Clicking a parcel breaks its tax down by jurisdiction and by land vs. structure. It also estimates what a mill levy would raise in a district, a drawn shape, or an uploaded shapefile.
