summary: >
  An AI tool from Beacon that estimates engineering hours from a transportation agency's own project history. It reads the agency's records, finds comparable past projects, and returns task-level hours by discipline. Every number links to its source. The arithmetic runs in a separate deterministic layer, so figures are calculated, not predicted. Georgia DOT piloted it on roundabout concept development. In August 2026, GDOT signed an agreement through 2031 to expand it to more project types across its 10-million-document ProjectWise archive.
